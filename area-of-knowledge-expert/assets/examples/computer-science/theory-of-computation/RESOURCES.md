# Resources — Theory of Computation

### sipser2013

- Type: BOOK
- Citation: SIPSER, M. **Introduction to the theory of computation**. 3. ed. Boston: Cengage Learning, 2013.
- URL: none
- Why: The standard first textbook; clear proofs, short chapters. Start here.

### turing1937

- Type: PAPER
- Citation: TURING, A. M. On computable numbers, with an application to the Entscheidungsproblem. **Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society**, v. s2-42, n. 1, p. 230-265, 1937.
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-42.1.230
- Why: The founding paper of computability. Read after Sipser's chapter on Turing machines.
